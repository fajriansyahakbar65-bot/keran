Record a tap outage

A registered household reports when its tap stops and when it flows again. Personal details stay private and only aggregated hours per kelurahan are published.

Verify each report

Check A is automatic (at least two neighbouring connections in the same kelurahan report an overlapping interval). Check B is the RT head confirmation. Check C compares the report with the operator's distribution schedule and pressure log. A report that passes A and B but conflicts with C is published as a discrepancy flag for the Ombudsman.

Outage Hours Index

OHI = verified outage hours in the last 7 days divided by registered connections in the kelurahan. Colour shows the longest verified ongoing outage against the 12 h and 24 h thresholds.

Claims triggered by verified evidence

At 12 h KERAN opens a tanker request with a public response clock. At 24 h it drafts a priority claim for SP4N-LAPOR!, addressed to SPAM Batam with a copy to Ombudsman Kepri.

Prototype for the essay "KERAN, Turning Household Tap Outages into Enforceable Water Priority" (IAWC 2026). All records are simulated for demonstration. Kelurahan names follow areas named in Ombudsman Kepri reports (2026). Thresholds follow Defra (2025) and Ombudsman Kepri (2026a).
